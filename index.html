HELO-SWAMPS

Clean Water. Higher Scores.

Pilot a helicopter over the swamp. Clean contaminated ponds, deliver water to village communities for cash, pay $10 to refuel, collect health crates, and dodge enemy helicopters. Score as many points as you can before your health or fuel runs out.

Difficulty

Hard adds more obstacles, extra enemy helicopters, and faster fuel drain.

Controls

  • WASD / Arrow Keys — Move
  • Space — Clean, deliver, or refuel
  • P — Pause
